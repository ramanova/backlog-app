Delete your Epoch Tasks account

Epoch Tasks: To-Do & Reminders, by ramanova

You only have an Epoch Tasks account if you turned on Cloud Sync and signed in, or connected an AI assistant by signing in. Without one, all your tasks are on your phone only, and uninstalling the app deletes them.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Epoch Tasks and go to Settings → Cloud Sync.
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away. The tasks on your phone stay there unless you remove them.

Without the app

Email kopiter2@gmail.com from the email address of the Google or Apple account you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my Epoch Tasks account”. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your synced tasks, tags, notes, due dates and reminders
  • Photos and files attached to your tasks
  • Your AI-assistant connections and personal links (they stop working immediately)
  • Your sign-in: account identifier, name, email address and profile picture address

What is kept, and for how long

  • Server logs (time, IP address, errors of each request) are deleted automatically within 30 days.
  • Copies in Google Cloud's internal backups are removed on Google's regular schedule.
  • The deleted account's random identifier and the deletion date are kept, so a device or assistant that was still signed in can't bring the account back. They aren't stored with your name, email address or any tasks.
  • Nothing else is kept.

See also the Privacy Policy.
